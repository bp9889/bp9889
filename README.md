### 🔭 Now building

**[Ashol Daam (আসল দাম)](https://github.com/bp9889/Ashol-Daam)** — is that Daraz discount real? Paste a product link and get a verdict in seconds: real deal, fair price or fake discount. It compares the same item across sellers (price + delivery), checks its price history twice a day, and flags prices raised right before a sale.

`FastAPI` · `PostgreSQL (Neon)` · `Playwright` · `GitHub Actions` · `Vercel`  —  **[Try it live →](https://ashol-daam.vercel.app)**
